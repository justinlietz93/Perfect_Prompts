# Writing Style Quick Reference

## Public default
**The AI Tipping Point is the primary public / Medium exemplar.**

Target feeling:

> distinctive idea -> strong hook -> intellectual motion -> accessible explanation -> widening consequence -> memorable close

- Give the reader a reason to want the next paragraph.
- Prefer a genuinely distinctive frame over a competent generic treatment.
- Historical, literary, mythic, and philosophical framing are welcome when they belong to the object.
- Keep technical density below research-paper density unless the article is explicitly a technical explainer.
- Let papers, tutorial notebooks, code, data, Lean, and appendices carry the deeper verification burden.
- Sources support the ride; they should not turn the article into a literature review.
- Each section should resolve something and expose the next question.

## Argumentative default
**Curiosity is Not a Soft Virtue is the primary argumentative exemplar.**

- Open with a memorable concrete case, reversal, or source discovery.
- State the conceptual distinction cleanly.
- Build persuasion through examples and source-led motion, not technical density.
- Short fragments can carry pressure.
- Do not fake balance when the evidence is asymmetric.
- Let the reader feel the argument being discovered rather than prosecuted.

## Technical explainer (specialized, not default Medium voice)
- Role before jargon.
- Plain language beside exact language.
- One or a few discriminating figures / measurements.
- Explain what each gate means.
- Link outward for full derivations, logs, proof, datasets, or validation packages.
- Do not mimic paper structure just to look rigorous.

## Fiction
- Scene works as life first.
- Sensory detail is causal, not decorative.
- Syntax belongs to POV.
- Do not explain the theme.
- Project law outranks generic advice.

## Poetry
- Physical image carries abstraction.
- Transform the relation.
- Repetition returns changed.
- Prefer compression over explanation.
- Rhyme is optional.

## Reject
- generic AI openings
- generic grandeur with no specific object
- research-paper mimicry in Medium articles
- jargon walls
- corporate neutrality
- unsupported certainty
- false caution
- dense output / methodology that belongs in a linked artifact
- thesis speeches in fiction
- explaining the poem after the image already did the work
