# Structure Method

Use three planning scales.

## 1. Macro movements

These are large changes in the novel's mode, pressure, relationship state, reader model, or central responsibility.

Do not force acts if movements describe the book better.

For each movement define:

- surface responsibility
- dominant relationships
- reader state
- protagonist state
- pressure that accumulates
- transition condition into the next movement

## 2. Mezzo sequences / chapters

A chapter is a container, not a quota.

Each sequence/chapter should know:

- why it exists now
- what concretely happens
- what changes by the end
- which character relationship changes
- what the reader now believes
- what pressure is carried forward

## 3. Microbeats

Microbeats may come from motifs, source artifacts, research, a playlist, memories, recurring objects, thematic questions, or other pressure fields.

A microbeat does **not** need its own scene. It can govern:

- a transition
- an image
- an interaction
- an absence
- a sentence turn
- an object
- a rhythm change
- a decision

## Surface plot test

Before adding structural complexity, ask:

**Would the ordinary surface story still be worth reading?**

If not, repair the human story before adding architecture.

## Uncertainty rule

Do not create one master outline, timeline, or explanation before the novel has earned one. Maintain multiple locally valid possibilities when the project depends on unresolved structure.
