# What the source novel's workflow is actually doing

This template was built after inspecting the canonical planning pack, its workbook, its drafting/checklist documents, the reference-field document, the frame lock, and drafted Chapter One/Chapter Two material.

## 1. It locks the artistic law before it locks the plot

The project begins by defining what kind of experience the novel is allowed to become, what interpretations/moves would collapse that experience, and what must remain conserved. Plot remains provisional much longer.

**Reusable principle:** lock identity before detail.

## 2. It treats the reader as a designed participant

The planning does not track only what happens to the protagonist. It tracks what the reader trusts, assumes, notices, repairs, and eventually recognizes.

**Reusable principle:** every serious novel benefits from a reader-experience arc, even when it has no twist or formal experiment.

## 3. It uses references by craft function rather than plot resemblance

The reference field asks what an audience is made to assume, what mechanism creates the effect, when that assumption changes, and what remains transferable after surface details are removed.

**Reusable principle:** metabolize mechanisms; do not collage plots.

## 4. It plans at three scales

- **Macro:** large movements in reader/protagonist state.
- **Mezzo:** chapters and scene sequences that function as ordinary life.
- **Micro:** pressure beats derived from a source field (in the source project, a 221-track playlist).

**Reusable principle:** preserve fine-grained source pressure without requiring every source item to become a scene.

## 5. It drafts trusted source/anchor scenes before solving the whole book

The early childhood scenes are not summaries of the architecture. They are proof that the book can live at scene level. Chapter One and Chapter Two show the method working: ordinary sensory causality and child-specific cognition carry the deeper pressure without the child becoming an adult philosopher.

**Reusable principle:** draft proof scenes early; let prose teach planning.

## 6. It separates complex streams before interleaving them

Where the novel contains divergent frames/timelines, the method drafts each stream locally first, then interleaves them. This protects local coherence.

**Reusable principle:** compose complexity in locally valid units before editing them into global complexity.

## 7. It uses explicit ledgers

The workbook tracks characters, settings, frames/timelines, chapter containers, scene cards, memory anchors, continuity, motifs, source beats, and revision tests.

**Reusable principle:** use a ledger when memory is a bad interface for the problem. Do not make the prose carry planning metadata.

## 8. It revises with falsifiable tests

The project asks concrete questions: does the scene work as life, is the reader being trained to solve too early, does a relief matter locally, is a contradiction intentional, does anyone explain the thesis, does the ending violate the law?

**Reusable principle:** revision gates should have pass/fail conditions, not only aesthetic feelings.

## 9. It preserves provenance and a canonical root

The project has one active canonical pack, archives older versions, keeps a changelog, and tries to establish a supersession order.

**Reusable principle:** creative projects also suffer from configuration drift. Treat the current creative state as something that needs version discipline.

# What the reusable template improves

The source pack also exposes a few workflow weaknesses worth fixing generically.

## A. Authority drift

The strongest later Frame Lock explicitly rejects certain framings, while older workbook language still contains some of those framings. Both can coexist in a large pack unless authority is explicit.

**Fix:** the reusable template establishes a hard authority stack: Frame Lock → Current Status → active workbook → method docs → archive.

## B. Scaffold text can masquerade as canon

A detailed workbook can make placeholders look decided simply because every row has content.

**Fix:** every meaningful decision uses PROPOSED / ACTIVE / LOCKED / DEPRECATED status language. Blank or provisional is better than invented certainty.

## C. The outline can become more authoritative than the prose

The source project's drafted chapters are more alive and specific than the early scaffold. A rigid process could mistakenly force those discoveries back into old planning assumptions.

**Fix:** after each substantial draft, prose updates the bible. Strong prose may invalidate weak outline assumptions.

## D. Voice needs its own lock

The source workflow has excellent structural safeguards, but its actual prose demonstrates a separate asset: a particular resolution of attention and consciousness.

**Fix:** the reusable pack adds `04A_VOICE_AND_PROSE_CALIBRATION.md` so revision cannot polish away the discovered voice.

## E. Not every novel needs the source project's specialized machinery

Ghost/dream frames, childhood memory anchors, an undecidable ontology, and a playlist ledger are project-specific.

**Fix:** the template retains the abstract tools but makes them optional: continuity can mean chronology, relationship knowledge, historical facts, mystery evidence, or viewpoint boundaries; the source ledger can be a playlist, notebook, archive, research corpus, or nothing at all.

# The reusable workflow

**Project seed**
→ **Frame Lock**
→ **Reader Experience Arc**
→ **Reference Field**
→ **Anchor Scenes**
→ **Voice Calibration**
→ **Character/World Surface**
→ **Macro Movements**
→ **Chapter/Sequence Blueprint**
→ **Scene Cards + Ledgers**
→ **Draft**
→ **Prose-to-bible feedback**
→ **Revision Gates**
→ **Canonical milestone**

The central discipline is simple:

**A mechanism never gets to excuse a dead scene, and a beautiful scene never gets to silently rewrite the book's governing law. When they conflict, stop and decide which one is wrong.**
