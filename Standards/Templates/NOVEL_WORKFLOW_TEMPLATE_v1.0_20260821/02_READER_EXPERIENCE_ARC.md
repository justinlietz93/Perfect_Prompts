# Reader Experience Arc

The protagonist has an arc. The **reader also has an arc**.

Plan what the reader believes, feels, expects, and notices at each major stage. This prevents a structurally clever novel from accidentally teaching the reader to read it in the wrong mode.

## Reader trust target

What must the reader care about before the novel asks them to carry greater structural or emotional complexity?

## Reader assumption target

What assumptions will arise naturally from the surface story?

Do not manufacture assumptions through deception when ordinary reading conventions will create them on their own.

## Recognition points

List moments where the reader's *model* of the book changes. A recognition point is not automatically a twist.

## Post-recognition obligation

What does the novel still need to do after its largest recognition/reveal/turn? Never assume recognition itself is an ending.
