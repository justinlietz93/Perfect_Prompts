# Claim Audit Pointer

For research-grade claims, use the canonical Research & Evidence Standard and its claim-audit machinery.

At minimum, a curriculum transfer should not blur:

- definition;
- theorem;
- conjecture;
- computation;
- experiment;
- interpretation;
- structural analogy;
- visual resemblance.

Finite evidence does not become an all-depth or universal theorem merely because the result is compelling.
