# Current Work Alignment

This folder maps the durable curriculum to live projects, research questions, professional tasks, or near-term goals.

It is intentionally lower authority than the master roadmap.

## Rule

Current work may reprioritize or activate permitted learning. It may not silently rewrite the prerequisite graph or declare an unearned gate satisfied.

## Files

- `ACTIVE_QUESTIONS.md` — current questions or tasks that may drive targeted learning.
- `TRANSFER_MAP.md` — maps current targets to curriculum groups.
